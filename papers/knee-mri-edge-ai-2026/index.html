Multimodal Knee MRI Diagnostics: Distilling Competition Ensembles into Offline On-Device Edge AI

RanjithKumar Ragavan

Independent Technical Report · TR-2026-01 · 2026/10/26

Download Full Paper (PDF) GitHub Repository ↗

Abstract

We present an end-to-end multimodal ensemble and on-device distillation workflow developed for multi-series knee MRI abnormality detection across 12 clinical endpoints (Sagittal, Coronal, and Axial sequences). Across 84 versioned architectures and 50+ scored evaluations in the RSNA Knee Abnormality Detection benchmark (6,425 participants), the ensemble achieved 0.942 Private Macro ROC AUC and 0.958 Public AUC before distillation and quantization into an offline, privacy-preserving mobile clinical scanner.
